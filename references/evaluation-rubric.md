# Humanize Writing Evaluation Rubric

Use a 0–4 scale. Scores describe the output, not the effort spent producing it.

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meaning preservation | claims are added/dropped or meaning reverses | mostly aligned but one material ambiguity or drift | every claim, scope, modality, and negation is aligned |
| Factual grounding | invented or unsupported output claims | source claims preserved but flags are incomplete | no additions; suspicious source claims are explicitly flagged |
| Language correctness | frequent errors obstruct reading | understandable with several errors or inconsistencies | grammar, spelling, punctuation, and register are clean |
| Naturalness and voice | mechanical, generic, or awkward | readable but uneven or still formulaic | sounds like one specific human writer |
| AI-pattern reduction | strong clusters remain | some justified or repeated tells remain | no unjustified structural clusters; vocabulary is concrete |
| Audience/purpose fit | wrong reader or delayed point | generally suitable but over-explains | direct, appropriately scoped, and context-aware |
| Completeness/concision | important content missing or padded | minor redundancy or compression issues | all necessary content remains with no empty scaffolding |

Use 1 and 3 for intermediate cases. Do not average away a guardrail failure.
